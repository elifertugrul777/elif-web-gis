How to read this atlas

A pin may indicate an author’s birthplace, a documented teaching or writing location, or a representative point for a language. Each card states which. Dates describe the evidence available; a dictionary or publication date does not necessarily identify when an expression was invented. Translations and reflection prompts are editorial, not extra historical quotations.

Traditional expressions can be used in many countries. Representative city pins do not claim exclusive ownership or a verified origin.
